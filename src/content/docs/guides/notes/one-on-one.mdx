One-on-ones are the single most important recurring meeting you have with your team. They're where trust is built, where small problems get surfaced before they become big ones, and where growth conversations actually happen.

A well-run 1:1 helps you:

- **Build trust over time**: Consistency matters more than any single meeting. Showing up prepared signals that you take the relationship seriously.
- **Catch issues early**: Frustrations, blockers, and doubts rarely arrive in a Slack message. They come out in a quiet room with the right question.
- **Support growth**: Careers are shaped in 1:1s, through feedback, coaching, and conversations about what's next.
- **Stay aligned**: A few minutes spent on priorities and context prevents hours of rework later.

The goal isn't to run through a checklist; it's to have a focused, honest conversation. EM Kit gives you the prep and follow-up scaffolding so you can spend the meeting itself actually listening.

#### Create a 1:1

1. Open **My Team** from the left sidebar.
2. Select a team member.
3. Open the **One-on-Ones** tab.
4. Click **New 1:1**.

#### What you can do in a 1:1

- Set the meeting **date**.
- Track the team member **mood**.
- Build a checkbox-based **Agenda**.
- Review **Feedbacks To Share** and mark items as shared.
- Manage **Action Items** with assignee and priority.
- Open **Recent Pull Requests or Jira Issues** for the member with filters and grouping.
- Review **Objectives** that overlap the meeting date.
- Write **Meeting Notes** in markdown.

#### Best practices

- Add agenda items before the meeting and check them live.
- Convert outcomes into Action Items before ending the session. You can create AI automation for that.
- Use Feedbacks To Share to make feedback delivery consistent.
