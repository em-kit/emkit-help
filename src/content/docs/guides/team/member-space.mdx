## Tabs

The Member space has multiple tabs:
- **General**: view & edit member information, notably Github handle, Jira account email, ...
- **1:1s & Notes**: access all 1:1 meetings and notes related to the member
- **Objectives**: view and manage the member's objectives and OKRs
- **Performance reviews**: access performance review forms and history for the member
- **Work Log**: view the member's work log entries
- **Feedbacks**: view feedbacks for the member
- **Career**: view the member's career path and aspirations
