Configure your team in EM Kit by adding members and managing their profiles. The Team page is where you can see all your team members at a glance, add new members, and access individual member spaces.

Go to **My Team**.

<Picture 
    src={TeamImage} 
    alt="Team Page"
/>

## Adding members

Click on **Add Member**. 
Add name, email, job level, birth date and start date in company then click on **Create**.

<Picture 
    src={AddMemberImage} 
    alt="Add Member"
/>

You will reach the member details page where you can add more information about the member.
