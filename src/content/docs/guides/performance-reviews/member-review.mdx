## Open a member review

You can open a member review from:

- **Performance -> Team Summary**
- **My Team -> Member -> Performance Reviews**

<Picture 
    src={PerformanceReviewImage} 
    alt="Performance Review - Member Review"
/>

## Header controls

At the top of the review page, you can set:

- **Status**
- **Rating**: configurable in review period settings

## Review tabs

### Overview

Write review notes and list highlights for impact and improvement.

### Objectives Checkin

Review objectives that overlap the review dates.

### Peer Feedbacks

Capture structured peer inputs (impact and improvement areas).

### Evidences

Work log for the review period with ability to scan for missed work log entries.

## Recommended flow

1. Set status and rating only after evidence is complete.
2. Review work log for the review period.
3. Add peer feedback before final synthesis in Overview notes.
