#### Create a review period

1. Click **New Review Period**.
2. Fill in:
- **Title** (for example, `H1 2026`)
- **Date Range**
3. Click **Create**.

<Picture 
    src={CreateReviewPeriodImage} 
    alt="Create Review Period"
/>


#### Edit or delete a period

- Click **Edit Period** to update title, dates, rating levels and template for review notes

<Picture 
    src={EditReviewPeriodImage} 
    alt="Edit Review Period"
/>

- Click **Delete Period** to remove the period and associated member review data.

#### Team Summary table

For the selected period, EM Kit shows:

- Team members
- Review status
- Rating

Click a row to open the member review when available.
