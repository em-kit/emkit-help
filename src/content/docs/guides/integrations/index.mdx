## Use cases

- **GitHub** and **Jira** power pull request and ticket context inside **One-on-Ones** and **Daily Meeting Notes**, and help you scan for missed work log entries when preparing **Performance Reviews**.
- **Slack** is used exclusively by the **EM Kit Copilot** so AI-assisted features can reach into Slack conversations through MCP.
- **Calendar** surfaces upcoming meetings on the **Home** page and lets you link **1:1 notes** to the calendar events they belong to.

## Configure an integration

1. Open **Settings -> Integrations**.
2. Click **Settings** on the integration card you want to configure.
3. Fill in the drawer form and click **Save**.
4. Click **Test** to verify the connection.

Integration status (Connected, Error, or not configured) is shown on each card.
